Privacy Policy

Lucarne · Last updated November 2, 2026

Lucarne is self-hosted software. It runs entirely on hardware owned by the person who installs it, usually a small computer behind a touchscreen in their home. The Lucarne project does not run any servers and does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

Google user data Lucarne accesses

Lucarne only accesses Google data when you connect a Google account yourself. It requests:

  • Basic profile and email address, to label which Google account is connected.
  • Google Calendar, to show events from the calendars you choose and to create, edit or delete events you change on the Lucarne screen.
  • Google Photos Picker (selected items only), to download the specific photos you pick, for use as a screensaver. Lucarne cannot see the rest of your photo library.

How the data is used and stored

  • All data, including OAuth tokens, calendar events and downloaded photos, is stored only in a local database and media folder on the device running Lucarne.
  • Data is used only to provide Lucarne's own features to the people in the home where it is installed.
  • Lucarne does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Data is sent only to Google's own APIs, and only to sync the calendars and photos you have selected.

Lucarne's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Optional services

If the person running Lucarne adds a Gemini API key, their meal-search words are sent to Google's Gemini API to find recipe pages, and the text of recipe pages without structured recipe data may be sent to Gemini to extract the recipe. When importing a recipe or checking meal-search results, Lucarne downloads the recipe page and its photo from that recipe website. Weather is fetched from Open-Meteo using the configured location. No Google Calendar or Google Photos data is sent to any of these services.

Removing your data

You can disconnect a Google account at any time in Lucarne's Settings, which revokes its access token and deletes the synced data from the device. You can also revoke access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting Lucarne's data folder removes everything it stored.
